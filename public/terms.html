TokenEconomy

13. Universal Token Economy & Balance Terms

Strict Utility Nature: Tokens represent internal digital utility credits used strictly to access computational workflows, specialized agent tasks, and educational services. Tokens are not legal tender, currency, securities, or financial investments.
  • Strict Integer Rule: Token balances (token_balance) and debits are strictly whole positive integers (Math.round). Decimal fractions of tokens are strictly disallowed by system invariant rules.
  • Purchase Increments: Tokens are purchased in increments of 10. Single checkout orders are capped at 1,000,000,000 tokens to prevent accidental inputs. Financial limits are bound between AUD/USD $0.50 and $999,999.99 per transaction.
  • Dynamic Geolocation Pricing: Visitors located in Australia (AU) are billed in Australian Dollars (AUD). Visitors connecting from outside Australia are billed in United States Dollars (USD). AUD pricing is never shown outside Australia.
  • Idempotent Debit Invariant: Spending tokens (POST /api/tokens/spend) requires an application tag and purchase reference (spend_{app}_{reference}). Duplicate requests are rejected, preventing double debits. Balances cannot fall below zero.
  • Non-Refundability: Tokens are credited immediately upon atomic database commit; token purchases are non-refundable and non-exchangeable for fiat cash, except where mandatory statutory refund rights apply under the Australian Consumer Law.
