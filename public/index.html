About David Robertson

David Robertson is an Australian AI and digital transformation specialist, university lecturer, and founder of the AI Foundation. He holds an MBA in Digital Transformation, a Master's degree in Applied Linguistics, and is a certified Australian Government Trainer and Assessor (TAE40110) currently completing Higher Degree by Research (HDR) thesis work in AI for education and training.

As a university convenor specializing in business applications for artificial intelligence and agentic systems, his work bridges technical engineering and operational strategy by designing custom, vendor-agnostic AI agent ecosystems, structured data pipelines, and practical automation tools. Over a multi-decade career spanning Australia, Japan, and Southeast Asia, Robertson has spearheaded digital transformation across corporate, higher education, medical regulatory, and small-business sectors.

Currently developing an AI Centre in Bangkok, he focuses on scaling agentic AI capabilities, practical business automation, and workforce digital skills across Southeast Asia.
